The vector product

Vector (cross) product

\[\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = |\mathbf{a}|\,|\mathbf{b}|\sin\theta\ \hat{\mathbf{n}}\] where \(\hat{\mathbf{n}}\) is the unit vector perpendicular to both \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\), given by the right-hand rule. The answer is a vector.

Size

\(|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}| = |\mathbf{a}|\,|\mathbf{b}|\sin\theta\) is the area of the parallelogram with sides \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\).

\(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\) is perpendicular to both vectors

Calculating it

Components

\[\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = (a_2b_3 - a_3b_2)\mathbf{i} - (a_1b_3 - a_3b_1)\mathbf{j} + (a_1b_2 - a_2b_1)\mathbf{k}\]

As a determinant

\[\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ a_1 & a_2 & a_3 \\ b_1 & b_2 & b_3 \end{vmatrix}\]

Always check

Your answer must give zero when dotted with \(\mathbf{a}\) and with \(\mathbf{b}\).

Properties

  • \(\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{a} = -(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b})\): order matters
  • \(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{a} = \mathbf{0}\), and \(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0}\) for parallel vectors
  • \(\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{c}\)
  • \((k\mathbf{a}) \times \mathbf{b} = k(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b})\)
  • \(\mathbf{i} \times \mathbf{j} = \mathbf{k},\quad \mathbf{j} \times \mathbf{k} = \mathbf{i},\quad \mathbf{k} \times \mathbf{i} = \mathbf{j}\)
Area of a triangle

Area of triangle \(ABC = \tfrac12\left|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}\right|\)

I do Calculating a vector product

Example 1

Find \(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\) for \(\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}\).

  1. 1 \(\mathbf{i}\): \((-1)(-1) - (3)(2) = -5\)
  2. 2 \(\mathbf{j}\): \(-\left[(2)(-1) - (3)(1)\right] = 5\)
  3. 3 \(\mathbf{k}\): \((2)(2) - (-1)(1) = 5\)
  4. ✓ \(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = -5\mathbf{i} + 5\mathbf{j} + 5\mathbf{k}\)
Check

\(\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}) = -10 - 5 + 15 = 0\) ✓   \(\mathbf{b}\cdot(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}) = -5 + 10 - 5 = 0\) ✓

Find \(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\) for \(\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}\).

  1. 1 \(\mathbf{i}\): \((3)(1) - (-2)(-1) = 1\)
  2. 2 \(\mathbf{j}\): \(-\left[(1)(1) - (-2)(2)\right] = -5\)
  3. 3 \(\mathbf{k}\): \((1)(-1) - (3)(2) = -7\)
  4. ✓ \(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} - 5\mathbf{j} - 7\mathbf{k}\)

I do Area of a triangle

Example 2

Find the exact area of the triangle with vertices \(A(1, 0, 2)\), \(B(3, 1, 1)\) and \(C(2, -1, 4)\).

  1. 1 \(\overrightarrow{AB} = 2\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC} = \mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\)
  2. 2 \(\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = (2 - 1)\mathbf{i} - (4 + 1)\mathbf{j} + (-2 - 1)\mathbf{k} = \mathbf{i} - 5\mathbf{j} - 3\mathbf{k}\)
  3. 3 \(\left|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}\right| = \sqrt{1 + 25 + 9} = \sqrt{35}\)
  4. ✓ Area \(= \dfrac{\sqrt{35}}{2}\)

We do Area of a triangle

Your turn, with me

Find the exact area of the triangle with vertices \(A(0, 1, 1)\), \(B(2, 2, 0)\) and \(C(1, 3, 3)\).

  1. 1 \(\overrightarrow{AB} = 2\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\)
  2. 2 \(\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = (2 + 2)\mathbf{i} - (4 + 1)\mathbf{j} + (4 - 1)\mathbf{k} = 4\mathbf{i} - 5\mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}\)
  3. 3 Magnitude \(= \sqrt{16 + 25 + 9} = \sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt2\)
  4. ✓ Area \(= \dfrac{5\sqrt2}{2}\)

I do Vector product algebra

Example 3

Simplify \((\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) \times (\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b})\).

  1. 1 Expand, keeping the order: \(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{b}\)
  2. 2 \(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{0}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0}\)
  3. 3 \(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{a} = -(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b})\)
  4. ✓ \((\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) \times (\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}) = -2(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b})\)
Careful

Never swap the order without changing the sign.

We do Vector product algebra

Your turn, with me

Simplify \((2\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) \times (\mathbf{a} - 3\mathbf{b})\).

  1. 1 \(2(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{a}) - 6(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}) + \mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{a} - 3(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{b})\)
  2. 2 \(= -6(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}) - (\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b})\)
  3. ✓ \(-7(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b})\)

I do Area, then a distance Exam style

Example 4

The points \(A(1, 1, 0)\), \(B(3, 2, 2)\) and \(C(2, 4, 1)\) are given. Find the area of triangle \(ABC\), and hence the distance from \(C\) to the line \(AB\).

  1. 1 \(\overrightarrow{AB} = 2\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC} = \mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}\)
  2. 2 \(\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = (1 - 6)\mathbf{i} - (2 - 2)\mathbf{j} + (6 - 1)\mathbf{k} = -5\mathbf{i} + 5\mathbf{k}\)
  3. 3 Area \(= \tfrac12\sqrt{50} = \dfrac{5\sqrt2}{2}\)
  4. 4 Area \(= \tfrac12 \times \text{base} \times \text{height}\) with base \(|\overrightarrow{AB}| = 3\)
  5. ✓ Area \(= \dfrac{5\sqrt2}{2}\) and distance \(= \dfrac{2 \times \text{area}}{3} = \dfrac{5\sqrt2}{3}\)
"Hence"

The distance is the height of the triangle when \(AB\) is the base.

Your turn, with me

The points \(A(1, 0, 1)\), \(B(2, 2, 3)\) and \(C(3, 1, 1)\) are given. Find the area of triangle \(ABC\), and hence the distance from \(C\) to the line \(AB\).

  1. 1 \(\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC} = 2\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j}\)
  2. 2 \(\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = (0 - 2)\mathbf{i} - (0 - 4)\mathbf{j} + (1 - 4)\mathbf{k} = -2\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j} - 3\mathbf{k}\)
  3. 3 Area \(= \tfrac12\sqrt{4 + 16 + 9} = \dfrac{\sqrt{29}}{2}\), and \(|\overrightarrow{AB}| = 3\)
  4. ✓ Area \(= \dfrac{\sqrt{29}}{2}\) and distance \(= \dfrac{\sqrt{29}}{3}\)

Find \((3\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{k}) \times (\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j})\).

\(-2\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} + 6\mathbf{k}\)

Find a unit vector perpendicular to both \(\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j}\) and \(\mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}\).

\((\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j})\times(\mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}) = \mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}\), so \(\tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k})\)

\(|\mathbf{a}| = 3\), \(|\mathbf{b}| = 4\) and \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = 6\). Find \(|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}|\).

\(\cos\theta = \tfrac12\), \(\sin\theta = \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\), so \(6\sqrt3\)
