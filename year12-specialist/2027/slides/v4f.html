How to write a vector proof

  • Draw the figure and choose an origin, usually a vertex or a centre
  • Name two (or three) vectors that everything else is built from
  • Translate what is given into vector equations
  • Compute the vectors in the statement to be proved
  • Conclude in words

Parallelogram \(OABC\): everything in terms of \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{c}\)

I do Diagonals of a parallelogram

Example 1

Prove that the diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.

  1. 1 Let \(OABC\) be a parallelogram with \(\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}\), \(\overrightarrow{OC} = \mathbf{c}\). Then \(\overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{c}\).
  2. 2 Midpoint of \(OB\): position vector \(\tfrac12(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{c})\)
  3. 3 Midpoint of \(AC\): position vector \(\tfrac12(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{c})\)
  4. ✓ The midpoints are the same point, so the diagonals bisect each other.

We do The midpoint theorem

Your turn, with me

In triangle \(OAB\), \(M\) and \(N\) are the midpoints of \(OA\) and \(OB\). Prove that \(MN\) is parallel to \(AB\) and half its length.

  1. 1 \(\overrightarrow{OM} = \tfrac12\mathbf{a}\) and \(\overrightarrow{ON} = \tfrac12\mathbf{b}\)
  2. 2 \(\overrightarrow{MN} = \tfrac12\mathbf{b} - \tfrac12\mathbf{a} = \tfrac12(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a})\)
  3. 3 \(\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}\), so \(\overrightarrow{MN} = \tfrac12\,\overrightarrow{AB}\)
  4. ✓ \(MN\) is parallel to \(AB\) and \(|\overrightarrow{MN}| = \tfrac12|\overrightarrow{AB}|\).

I do The angle in a semicircle

Example 2

\(AB\) is a diameter of a circle with centre \(O\), and \(P\) is another point on the circle. Prove that \(\angle APB = 90^\circ\).

  1. 1 Let \(\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}\), so \(\overrightarrow{OB} = -\mathbf{a}\). Let \(\overrightarrow{OP} = \mathbf{p}\), with \(|\mathbf{p}| = |\mathbf{a}|\) (radii).
  2. 2 \(\overrightarrow{AP} = \mathbf{p} - \mathbf{a}\) and \(\overrightarrow{BP} = \mathbf{p} + \mathbf{a}\)
  3. 3 \(\overrightarrow{AP}\cdot\overrightarrow{BP} = (\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{a})\cdot(\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{a}) = |\mathbf{p}|^2 - |\mathbf{a}|^2 = 0\)
  4. ✓ \(\overrightarrow{AP} \perp \overrightarrow{BP}\), so \(\angle APB = 90^\circ\).
Choice of origin

Putting \(O\) at the centre makes every radius the same length.

We do Equal diagonals

Your turn, with me

Prove that a parallelogram with diagonals of equal length is a rectangle.

  1. 1 \(OABC\) with \(\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}\), \(\overrightarrow{OC} = \mathbf{c}\). Diagonals: \(\overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{c}\), \(\overrightarrow{AC} = \mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a}\).
  2. 2 Given \(|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{c}|^2 = |\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a}|^2\)
  3. 3 \(|\mathbf{a}|^2 + 2\,\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c} + |\mathbf{c}|^2 = |\mathbf{c}|^2 - 2\,\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c} + |\mathbf{a}|^2\)
  4. 4 \(4\,\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c} = 0\), so \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c} = 0\)
  5. ✓ \(OA \perp OC\), so the parallelogram is a rectangle.

I do The parallelogram law Exam style

Example 3

\(OABC\) is a parallelogram. Prove that \(|\overrightarrow{OB}|^2 + |\overrightarrow{AC}|^2 = 2\left(|\overrightarrow{OA}|^2 + |\overrightarrow{OC}|^2\right)\).

  1. 1 \(\overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{c}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC} = \mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a}\)
  2. 2 \(|\overrightarrow{OB}|^2 = |\mathbf{a}|^2 + 2\,\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c} + |\mathbf{c}|^2\)
  3. 3 \(|\overrightarrow{AC}|^2 = |\mathbf{c}|^2 - 2\,\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c} + |\mathbf{a}|^2\)
  4. 4 Adding: the \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c}\) terms cancel.
  5. ✓ \(|\overrightarrow{OB}|^2 + |\overrightarrow{AC}|^2 = 2|\mathbf{a}|^2 + 2|\mathbf{c}|^2\), as required.

We do A median result Exam style

Your turn, with me

In triangle \(OAB\), \(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB\). Prove that \(|\overrightarrow{OA}|^2 + |\overrightarrow{OB}|^2 = 2|\overrightarrow{OM}|^2 + 2|\overrightarrow{AM}|^2\).

  1. 1 \(\overrightarrow{OM} = \tfrac12(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b})\) and \(\overrightarrow{AM} = \tfrac12(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a})\)
  2. 2 \(2|\overrightarrow{OM}|^2 = \tfrac12\left(|\mathbf{a}|^2 + 2\,\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} + |\mathbf{b}|^2\right)\)
  3. 3 \(2|\overrightarrow{AM}|^2 = \tfrac12\left(|\mathbf{b}|^2 - 2\,\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} + |\mathbf{a}|^2\right)\)
  4. ✓ Sum \(= |\mathbf{a}|^2 + |\mathbf{b}|^2\), as required.

You do Prove these

Prove that the diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular.

\((\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{c})\cdot(\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a}) = |\mathbf{c}|^2 - |\mathbf{a}|^2 = 0\) because the sides are equal.

In triangle \(OAB\), \(|\overrightarrow{OA}| = |\overrightarrow{OB}|\) and \(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB\). Prove \(OM \perp AB\).

\(\tfrac12(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b})\cdot(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}) = \tfrac12(|\mathbf{b}|^2 - |\mathbf{a}|^2) = 0\)

Prove that the midpoints of the sides of any quadrilateral form a parallelogram.

With midpoints \(P, Q, R, S\) of \(AB, BC, CD, DA\): \(\overrightarrow{PQ} = \tfrac12(\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a}) = \overrightarrow{SR}\)
