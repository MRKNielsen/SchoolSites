Resolving one vector along another

Scalar resolute

The signed length of the shadow of \(\mathbf{a}\) on \(\mathbf{b}\): \[\mathbf{a} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{b}} = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{b}|}\]

Vector resolute

The shadow itself, a vector parallel to \(\mathbf{b}\): \[\mathbf{u} = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{\mathbf{b}\cdot\mathbf{b}}\,\mathbf{b}\]

Perpendicular part

\(\mathbf{w} = \mathbf{a} - \mathbf{u}\). Always check that \(\mathbf{w} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 0\).

\(\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{u} + \mathbf{w}\)

I do Scalar and vector resolutes

Example 1

Let \(\mathbf{a} = 3\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\). Find the scalar resolute and the vector resolute of \(\mathbf{a}\) in the direction of \(\mathbf{b}\), and the vector resolute of \(\mathbf{a}\) perpendicular to \(\mathbf{b}\).

  1. 1 \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = 3 - 4 - 2 = -3\) and \(|\mathbf{b}| = 3\)
  2. 2 Scalar resolute \(= \dfrac{-3}{3} = -1\)
  3. 3 Vector resolute \(\mathbf{u} = \dfrac{-3}{9}\mathbf{b} = -\tfrac13(\mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k})\)
  4. 4 Perpendicular part \(\mathbf{w} = \mathbf{a} - \mathbf{u} = \tfrac13(10\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k})\)
  5. ✓ \(-1\);   \(-\tfrac13(\mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k})\);   \(\tfrac13(10\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k})\)
Check

\(\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{b} = \tfrac13(10 - 8 - 2) = 0\) ✓

Negative?

A negative scalar resolute means the shadow points opposite to \(\mathbf{b}\).

Your turn, with me

Let \(\mathbf{a} = 5\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\). Find the scalar resolute and the vector resolute of \(\mathbf{a}\) in the direction of \(\mathbf{b}\), and the vector resolute of \(\mathbf{a}\) perpendicular to \(\mathbf{b}\).

  1. 1 \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = 10 - 1 + 6 = 15\) and \(|\mathbf{b}| = 3\)
  2. 2 Scalar resolute \(= 5\)
  3. 3 \(\mathbf{u} = \tfrac{15}{9}\mathbf{b} = \tfrac53(2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k})\)
  4. 4 \(\mathbf{w} = \mathbf{a} - \mathbf{u} = \tfrac13(5\mathbf{i} + 8\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k})\)
  5. ✓ \(5\);   \(\tfrac53(2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k})\);   \(\tfrac13(5\mathbf{i} + 8\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k})\)

I do Distance from a point to a line through O

Example 2

Find the distance from the point \(A(4, 1, -2)\) to the line through the origin and \(B(2, 2, 1)\).

  1. 1 The distance is the length of the part of \(\mathbf{a}\) perpendicular to \(\mathbf{b}\).
  2. 2 \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = 8 + 2 - 2 = 8\), \(|\mathbf{b}| = 3\), so the scalar resolute is \(\tfrac83\)
  3. 3 \(|\mathbf{a}|^2 = 16 + 1 + 4 = 21\)
  4. 4 Pythagoras: distance\(^2 = 21 - \tfrac{64}{9} = \tfrac{125}{9}\)
  5. ✓ Distance \(= \dfrac{5\sqrt5}{3}\)
Shortcut

\(|\mathbf{w}|^2 = |\mathbf{a}|^2 - (\text{scalar resolute})^2\)

Your turn, with me

Find the distance from the point \(A(3, 0, 4)\) to the line through the origin and \(B(1, 2, 2)\).

  1. 1 \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = 3 + 0 + 8 = 11\), \(|\mathbf{b}| = 3\), scalar resolute \(= \tfrac{11}{3}\)
  2. 2 \(|\mathbf{a}|^2 = 25\)
  3. 3 distance\(^2 = 25 - \tfrac{121}{9} = \tfrac{104}{9}\)
  4. ✓ Distance \(= \dfrac{2\sqrt{26}}{3}\)

I do Working backwards from a resolute Exam style

Example 3

The vector resolute of \(\mathbf{a} = m\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}\) in the direction of \(\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\) is \(\tfrac23\mathbf{b}\). Find \(m\), and hence find the vector resolute of \(\mathbf{a}\) perpendicular to \(\mathbf{b}\).

  1. 1 Vector resolute \(= \dfrac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{\mathbf{b}\cdot\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{b}\), so \(\dfrac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{9} = \dfrac23\)
  2. 2 \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = 2m - 2 - 2 = 2m - 4 = 6\), so \(m = 5\)
  3. 3 \(\mathbf{w} = \mathbf{a} - \tfrac23\mathbf{b} = (5\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}) - \tfrac23(2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k})\)
  4. ✓ \(m = 5\) and \(\mathbf{w} = \tfrac13(11\mathbf{i} + 8\mathbf{j} - 7\mathbf{k})\)
Check

\(\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{b} = \tfrac13(22 - 8 - 14) = 0\) ✓

We do Working backwards from a resolute Exam style

Your turn, with me

The scalar resolute of \(\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{i} + m\mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\) in the direction of \(\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}\) is 2. Find \(m\).

  1. 1 \(\dfrac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{b}|} = 2\) and \(|\mathbf{b}| = 3\)
  2. 2 \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = 2 + 2m - 2 = 2m\)
  3. 3 \(\dfrac{2m}{3} = 2\)
  4. ✓ \(m = 3\)
