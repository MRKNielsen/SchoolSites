Staff blob tool

Encrypts a page’s content behind the staff password, and decrypts it back again for editing. Everything happens in this browser — the password is never sent anywhere and never stored. Used by solutions.html and rubric.html in the Year 7 Science ecosystems unit.

Keep plaintext out of the repo. After encrypting, delete the *-payload.html file you fed in — .gitignore stops it being committed by accident, but the file still sits in your working copy. To edit a gated page later, decrypt it here, change it, and encrypt it again.

Encrypt

Choose the plaintext payload — the HTML that should sit inside <div id="content"> once unlocked. You get back one line to paste over the var BLOB = … line in the gated page.

Decrypt

Choose a gated page (solutions.html, rubric.html) and it pulls the blob out, decrypts it, and hands back the plaintext payload to edit.
