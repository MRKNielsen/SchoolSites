Unit 1 Worksheets

Eight printable revision sheets, one per week of AOS 1. Away from the computer, from memory first, nothing marked.

How these differ from the workbooks

Both are required, and they do different jobs.

💻
Workbooks — at the computer, during the lesson
The fourth lesson of each week. You work on the real dataset in marimo and every exercise checks itself as you go. That is where the skill is built.
Lesson 4
✏
Worksheets — away from the computer, afterwards
Revision and consolidation. Written by hand, from memory first, then checked against your booklet in a different colour. Every sheet is self-contained: the data, the brief and the definitions you need are printed on the page beside the question.
Revision

The worksheets

Each one covers the week named on its card. Print it, or fill it in on screen and print the result.

1
The methodology, data and information
Topics 1–3 · The four stages of the problem-solving methodology; data against information; qualitative and quantitative; choosing a data type.
2
Data quality, sources and privacy
Topics 4–6 · Naming the fault in a bad row; accurate against reliable; where bias comes from; primary and secondary sources; APA references; APP 2, 6 and 11.
3
Ethics, requirements and design
Topics 7–9 · Legal against ethical; algorithmic bias; functional and non-functional requirements; the five kinds of constraint; reading a brief; IPO charts, query designs and mock-ups.
4
Databases, keys and relationships
Topics 10–11 · What redundancy costs; testing a primary key; composite keys; which side carries the foreign key; normalising a flat table; referential integrity and orphans.
5
SQL on paper
Topics 12–14 · Reading a query and writing its result; writing queries longhand; finding the fault in five broken ones; WHERE against HAVING; turning a query design into SQL.
6
Descriptive statistics
Topic 15 · Mean, median, mode and range by hand; what the gap between mean and median tells you; what an outlier does to each; spreadsheet formulas; what spread says that centre cannot.
7
Data visualisation
Topic 16 · Choosing the chart; drawing the same bars twice, once from zero and once truncated; fault-finding described charts; what every chart must carry.
8
Area of Study 1 — whole revision
Topics 1–16 · One unseen dataset taken from raw extract to finished chart, plus a brief to pull apart and an extended response. This is the shape of the Outcome 1 task.
