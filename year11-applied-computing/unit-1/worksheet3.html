Part CFive kinds of constraintTopic 8

Economic — cost and time. Technical — speed, capacity, equipment, compatibility, security. Social — the expertise your users actually have. Legal — intellectual property, ownership, privacy. Usability — accessibility, usefulness, ease of use.

Worksheet 3 — Ethics, requirements and designPart D · Topics 8–9
Part DReading a briefTopics 8–9

The brief below is the only information you have. Everything you need is in it.

The brief

The school’s sustainability group wants a one-page summary of Victorian rainfall for 2025, built from the Bureau of Meteorology daily records for six weather stations. It must show total rainfall by region and the wettest month, and it has to be understandable at a glance by students in Years 7 to 12. It will be built in a spreadsheet, because that is what is on the school laptops. It is due in two weeks. Forecasting is not wanted, and neither is temperature.

7
Pull the brief apart.
(a)Write two functional requirements from it.
(b)Write one non-functional requirement from it.
(c)Name two constraints, and give the kind of each.
(d)State two things that are explicitly out of scope.
(e)Name one thing the brief does not say, which you would have to ask about before building anything.
