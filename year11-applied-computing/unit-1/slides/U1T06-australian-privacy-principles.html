Scope of this lesson

There are thirteen Australian Privacy Principles. The study design names three. Know those three properly rather than all thirteen vaguely.

Starting point

What counts as personal information?

Information about an identified individual, or one who is reasonably identifiable.

That second half is the part people underestimate. A record with the name removed can still identify someone if the remaining fields narrow it far enough.

Discuss

A dataset holds no names — only postcode, date of birth and school year level. In a town of 4000 people, how many students share all three?

Key knowledge

APP 2, APP 6 and APP 11

PrincipleIn shortIn practice
APP 2
Anonymity		People may deal with you without identifying themselvesDo not collect a name you do not need. De-identify before you analyse
APP 6
Use and disclosure		Information held for one purpose may not be used for anotherSurvey data about canteen queues cannot become a marketing list
APP 11
Security		You must protect what you hold, and destroy it when donePasswords, access limits, and deleting data you no longer need

Worked example

I do A survey of canteen use

The scenario

You survey 200 students about canteen queues. The form asks for name, year level, how often they buy lunch, and how long they wait.

  1. 1 Do I need the name to answer the question? No. APP 2 says do not collect it.
  2. 2 Could the school use the answers to decide who gets a canteen job? That is a different purpose. APP 6 says no.
  3. 3 Where is the file saved? A shared drive anyone can open fails APP 11.
  4. ✓ When the report is finished, what happens to the raw responses? APP 11 covers destruction too.
The cheapest privacy control

Not collecting it. Every field you do not collect is one you cannot leak, misuse or have to protect.

3. A feedback form demands your full name to submit

Often more than one

The sports brand case is also an APP 11 failure, because emailing it was the disclosure. Name the clearest one, then say what else it touches.

Key skill

Removing the name is not de-identification

De-identification means a person cannot reasonably be re-identified — including by joining your data to something else.

Techniques that actually help:

  • Aggregate — report by year level, not by student
  • Band — store an age range, not a birth date
  • Suppress small groups — do not publish a cell containing two people
Why our dataset is weather

It carries no personal information at all, which is why it can sit in a public repository. That was a deliberate choice, and it is the kind of choice this principle asks you to make.
