Why design before building

Changing a mock-up takes a pencil. Changing a finished spreadsheet takes an afternoon. Design is where mistakes are cheap.

Worked example

I do What an annotation has to say

A weak annotation

“Title here. Chart. Some text.”

  1. 1 What size is the title, and in what typeface? Not stated — so the builder guesses.
  2. 2 Which chart? A bar and a line make different claims about the same numbers.
  3. 3 What text, and how much? “Some” is not a design decision.
  4. ✓ A good annotation names size, position, colour, typeface and content — enough that two people building from it produce the same thing.
The test for a design

Could somebody else build it from this without asking you a question? If not, it is a sketch, not a design.

Together

We do Make these specific

1. “Big heading at the top”

“Heading, 36 pt Bitter bold, centred, 20 mm from the top”

2. “Chart of the rainfall”

“Column chart, one column per region, rainfall in mm on the vertical axis”

3. “Use nice colours”

“One accent colour for all columns; grey axis labels; no colour used to mean two different things”
