Part CReading the briefTopics 7–9

The brief below is all you have.

The brief

The school council wants to know where its electricity goes. Using the sensor readings for 2025, produce a summary showing power used by campus and the single highest-using sensor. It must be readable by council members who do not work in schools, and it has to be built in a spreadsheet, because that is what the office has. Due at the end of term. Do not include costs — the business manager handles those — and do not predict next year.
