Worksheet 4 — Databases, keys and relationshipsPart E · Topic 11
Part EReferential integrityTopic 11

Referential integrity means the relationships actually hold: every foreign key points at a record that exists. In SQLite it is switched off until you switch it on.

9
An observation row records station_id = 99999, and no station with that id exists.
(a)Name the term for a record like this.
(b)Explain what happens to that row when you join observation to station, and say whether you would notice.
(c)State what you would have to turn on in SQLite to stop the row being accepted in the first place.
10
Deleting a region that still has stations in it would break referential integrity.
(a)Explain what would be wrong with the station records afterwards.
(b)Give two different things a database could reasonably do when someone tries that deletion.
