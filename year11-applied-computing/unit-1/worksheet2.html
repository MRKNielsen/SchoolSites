Worksheet 2 — Data quality, sources and privacyPart B · Topic 4
Part BAccurate is not reliableTopic 4

Accurate means close to the true value — a property of the reading. Reliable means dependable over time — a property of the source.

3
Two thermometers are tested against a laboratory standard every day for a month.
Thermometer A reads exactly 2 °C too high every single day.
Thermometer B is right on average, but on random days it is out by up to 6 °C.
(a)Which one is reliable? Which one is accurate on average? Answer both in one sentence each.
(b)One of the two can still be used for real work. Say which, and explain what you would do to its readings.
(c)Explain why the other one is useless, even though it is right on average.

Worksheet 2 — Data quality, sources and privacyPart E · Topic 6
Part EThe Australian Privacy PrinciplesTopic 6

There are thirteen Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988. Three of them are named in this course.

The three you need

APP 2 — Anonymity. People may deal with you without identifying themselves. Do not collect a name you do not need; de-identify before you analyse.
APP 6 — Use and disclosure. Information held for one purpose may not be used for another.
APP 11 — Security. You must protect what you hold, and destroy or de-identify it when you no longer need it.
