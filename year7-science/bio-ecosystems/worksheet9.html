Part DCountry before and after3 marks
First Nations perspectives

Before 1835, bettongs, quolls, bandicoots and bush stone-curlews were common across Wurundjeri-Woiwurrung Country. Sheep, cattle, rabbits, foxes, cats and blackberry arrived almost simultaneously, and within decades species that had persisted for millennia were locally extinct.
