Research Portfolio — Model Answers

Model responses and marking criteria for all eleven stages of the Victorian Biodiversity Research Portfolio, plus the front matter and the closing self-check. Every answer is sourced from the Eastern Barred Bandicoot species profile and the unit booklet — the two documents students are meant to be working from.

11 stages· At The Level + Above The Level· Unmarked — criteria, not a mark scheme· Mapped to CAT Parts A–G· Victorian Curriculum F–10 v2.0
How to use: The portfolio carries no marks — students judge their own work against the CAT criteria on the closing page. These are model responses and the criteria that separate a complete answer from a thin one, not the only acceptable wording. Several stages are open by design (the sketch, the conservation action, the self-check); those give criteria rather than an answer. Yr 8 marks the Above The Level extensions and First Nations the Stage 7 perspectives task.
