Part DNames that carry knowledge3 marks
First Nations perspectives

Wurun = the manna gum (Eucalyptus viminalis) that grows along the Birrarung. Djeri = the grub found in that tree. The name Wurundjeri records a relationship between a plant and the animal that depends on it.

8
3Compare this with the scientific name Eucalyptus viminalis. What extra information does the Wurundjeri name carry that the scientific name does not?
