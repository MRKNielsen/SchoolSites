Part DKnowledge systems on Country3 marks
First Nations perspectives

Long before Linnaeus, Wurundjeri-Woiwurrung people had detailed systems for naming and grouping organisms on Country — built over thousands of generations of observation, and including each organism’s seasonal behaviour and its relationships to other species.
