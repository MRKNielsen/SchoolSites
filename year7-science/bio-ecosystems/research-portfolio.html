How the stages map to the CAT

The Common Assessment Task has seven parts, A to G. Every stage feeds directly into one or more of them — if a CAT question is hard, go back to the stage beside it. Part F is the key criterion.

Where the facts come from
Students answer every stage from the Eastern Barred Bandicoot species profile, which is laid out in the same five sections as the Species Booklet issued in the CAT — Profile, Ecology, Food-web evidence, Threats, Conservation. The CAT itself uses a different species: what transfers is the method, not the animal.
