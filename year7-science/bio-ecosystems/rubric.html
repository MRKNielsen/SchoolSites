Task details

These appear in the header of every printed rubric.

Band labels

Band 0 is the “not demonstrated” column added to the printed rubric. The rubric as issued also has two columns both labelled “At expected standard” — rename them here if you want the printed sheet and the comments to distinguish the two.

Students

One per line. Add a species and pronoun after commas if you want them: Ava Chen, Regent Honeyeater, she

Marks already entered are kept for any student whose name is unchanged. Removing a name removes that student's marks.

Data

CSV opens in Excel or Google Sheets — one row per student, one column per booklet question, in booklet order. Put a 1 (or y) where the student was successful and an x where the question was not attempted. The Override columns are optional and accept 0–4.

Marking grid

Columns follow the Student Booklet question by question. Where the rubric splits one question across two bands it appears as (i) and (ii) under that question number. Click once for a successful answer, twice for not attempted, three times to clear. Bands are worked out from the ticks: a cell counts only when every question behind it is done, and each missing question below the top cell pulls the award down a band.
