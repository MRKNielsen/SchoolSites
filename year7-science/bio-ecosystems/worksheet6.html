Part DCultural burning and food webs3 marks
First Nations perspectives

Wurundjeri-Woiwurrung people used cultural burning — careful, seasonal, low-intensity fire timed by ecological knowledge — producing a mosaic of vegetation of different ages across Country: fresh growth, dense scrub and open ground side by side.
