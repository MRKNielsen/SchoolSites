Part BA Victorian success story6 marks
Eastern barred bandicoot

Declared extinct in the wild in Victoria in 1989 with about 150 animals left at Hamilton. Recovery used captive breeding at Zoos Victoria, predator-proof fenced enclosures at three sites, and predator control at reintroduction sites. The wild population is now over 1,500 and the species was reclassified to “endangered” in 2022.

Part DThe world’s oldest conservation system3 marks
First Nations perspectives

First Nations peoples have managed Country for ecosystem health for at least 65,000 years. Indigenous Protected Areas now cover more than 67 million hectares — the largest single category of protected area in Australia — and studies consistently find their biodiversity outcomes rival or exceed those of areas managed by conservation agencies.
