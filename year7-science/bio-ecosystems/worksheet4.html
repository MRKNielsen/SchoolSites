Part DIdentifying Country3 marks
First Nations perspectives

Wurundjeri-Woiwurrung people identify organisms on Country using sound, scent, tracks, behaviour and seasonal timing as well as what an organism looks like — for example identifying a frog by its call, or knowing that a particular plant flowering means a particular fish is running.

3A visual dichotomous key answers “what is this organism?”. Name one question that multi-sensory Indigenous ecological knowledge can answer that a key cannot, and explain why that matters to a field ecologist.
