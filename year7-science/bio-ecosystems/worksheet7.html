Part DCaring for Country3 marks
First Nations perspectives

Caring for Country — cultural burning, managing water flows, strict rules against over-harvesting, and seasonal movement across Country — maintained ecosystem services for tens of thousands of years. Modern research finds Country managed by First Nations peoples often has higher biodiversity and more intact services than land managed by other methods.
