Part DAdaptations as an ecological calendar3 marks
First Nations perspectives

Bogong moths (Agrotis infusa) migrate to the alps each spring and gather in rock crevices. Wurundjeri and other Kulin Nation peoples travelled to the alps for moth harvests — a rich, high-fat seasonal food — timing the journey by their knowledge of the moths’ behaviour.
