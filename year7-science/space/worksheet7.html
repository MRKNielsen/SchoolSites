Part BThe sky as a calendar9 marks

5When the Emu appears in a particular position at dusk, it signals that emus are laying. Explain how this works as a calendar, and why it is more precise than a fixed four-season European calendar for this Country.

4The Kulin seasonal calendar has six (some say seven) seasons rather than four. Give two ways a six-season calendar suits south-eastern Australia better than the four-season model.
