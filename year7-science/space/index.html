Space

Teacher slide decks for a 7-week unit on the solar system, stars, space exploration, gravity, light, Aboriginal astronomy, and the search for life.

11 lessons · Written assessment · Spaced retrieval quizzes built in · First Nations perspectives throughout
Acknowledgement of Country
We acknowledge the Wurundjeri-Woiwurrung people of the Kulin Nation as the Traditional Custodians of the Country on which we learn. We pay our respects to Elders past, present, and emerging. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander astronomical knowledge — developed over tens of thousands of years — is woven throughout this unit as living, ongoing knowledge, not as a historical artefact.

Lessons

Eleven slide decks. From lesson 2 each opens with a spaced retrieval quiz drawn at random from earlier lessons.

01
Earth, Moon & Sun — Scale & Structure
Sizes and distances, Earth’s layers, the Moon’s surface, the Sun’s structure and energy source.
02
Day, Night & Seasons
Earth’s rotation and revolution, axial tilt, Southern Hemisphere seasons, solstices and equinoxes.
⚡ Quiz: Lesson 1
03
The Moon — Phases & Tides
Lunar phases, the monthly cycle, spring and neap tides, solar and lunar eclipses.
⚡ Quiz: Lessons 1–2
04
Our Solar System
The 8 planets, dwarf planets, asteroid belt, Kuiper Belt, AU distances, and the scale of our neighbourhood.
⚡ Quiz: Lessons 1–3
05
Beyond the Solar System — Stars & Galaxies
Light-years, stellar classification, the Milky Way, nebulae, stellar life cycles, and the Big Bang.
⚡ Quiz: Lessons 1–4
06
Space Exploration — History & Technology
Sputnik to JWST, Apollo, Mars rovers, the ISS, Australia’s space contributions (Parkes, CSIRO, ASA).
⚡ Quiz: Lessons 1–5
07
Gravity & Orbits
Newton’s law of gravitation, orbital motion, Kepler’s laws, escape velocity, tidal locking.
⚡ Quiz: Lessons 1–6
08
Light from Space — Telescopes & the EM Spectrum
Electromagnetic spectrum, optical and radio telescopes, JWST, spectroscopy, interferometry, SKA.
⚡ Quiz: Lessons 1–7
09
Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander Astronomy
Dark constellations, the Emu in the Sky, Boorong knowledge, seasonal calendars, songlines, and two-way learning.
⚡ Quiz: Lessons 1–8
10
Space & Sustainability — Debris & Earth Observation
Space debris, Kessler syndrome, satellite uses (GPS, weather, Earth observation), managing orbital space.
⚡ Quiz: Lessons 1–9
11
The Search for Life — Astrobiology
Habitable zone, Mars, extremophiles, Europa and Enceladus, the Drake equation, SETI, and the Fermi paradox.
⚡ Quiz: Lessons 1–10
