Year 7 Science · Victorian Curriculum 2.0

Space

A 4-week teaching program on the Earth–Moon–Sun system, the South Australian rocks that record it, the solar system, space technology and Aboriginal astronomy — with three above-level lessons for extension.

6 core lessons + 1 optional + 3 extension · Written assessment · Spaced retrieval quizzes built in · Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander astronomy in Lesson 7
Acknowledgement of Country
We acknowledge the Wurundjeri-Woiwurrung people of the Kulin Nation as the Traditional Custodians of the Country on which we learn. We pay our respects to Elders past, present, and emerging. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander astronomical knowledge — developed over tens of thousands of years — is woven throughout this unit as living, ongoing knowledge, not as a historical artefact.

Contents

Each lesson’s deck, and the page it starts on in the printed booklet. Its worksheet sits underneath.

Week 1

Our place in space — scale, and why the sky repeats itself.

1 Earth, Moon & Sun — Scale & Structure Turn to page 2

Sizes and distances, Earth’s layers, the Moon’s surface, the Sun’s structure and energy source.

✎Worksheet 138 marks
2 Day, Night & Seasons Turn to page 8

Earth’s rotation and revolution, axial tilt, why Australia has summer in December, solstices and equinoxes.

⭐ Quiz: Lesson 1

✎Worksheet 236 marks

Week 2

The Moon, the evidence it leaves in rock, and out into the solar system.

3 The Moon — Phases & Tides Turn to page 11

Why the Moon changes shape, the eight phases, spring and neap tides, and eclipses.

⭐ Quiz: Lessons 1–2

✎Worksheet 334 marks
4 Optional · Specimens Reading the Rocks Turn to page 14

Tidal rhythmites that record a 21.9-hour day 635 million years ago, and the Acraman asteroid impact. Built on specimens and research posters from Prof. George Williams (University of Adelaide), held in the department.

⭐ Quiz: Lessons 1–3

✎Worksheet 434 marks
5 Our Solar System Turn to page 20

The 8 planets, dwarf planets, asteroid belt, Kuiper Belt, AU distances, and the scale of our neighbourhood.

⭐ Quiz: Lessons 1–3

✎Worksheet 535 marks

Week 3

What we send up, and tens of thousands of years of observation on Country.

6 Space Technology & Sustainability Turn to page 23

Two parts — Sputnik to JWST, Apollo, Mars rovers and Australia’s contributions; then satellites, debris, Kessler syndrome and Earth observation.

⭐ Quiz: Lessons 1–3, 5

✎Worksheet 631 marks
7 Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander Astronomy Turn to page 28

The unit’s Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander astronomy gathered in one lesson — dark constellations, the Emu in the Sky, the Boorong catalogue, seasonal calendars, celestial navigation, the SKA on Wajarri Country, and two-way learning.

⭐ Quiz: Lessons 1–3, 5–6

✎Worksheet 730 marks

Week 4 · Above level — extension

Everything past Lesson 7 sits above the Year 7 level. The core three weeks are complete without it — run these with a class that is ready, or set them as extension for individual students.

8 Above level The Search for Life — Astrobiology Turn to page 33

Habitable zone, Mars, extremophiles, Europa and Enceladus, the Drake equation, SETI, and the Fermi paradox.

⭐ Quiz: Lessons 1–3, 5–7

✎Worksheet 833 marks
9 Above level Gravity & Orbits Turn to page 38

Newton’s law of gravitation, orbital motion, orbital speed and altitude, escape velocity, tidal locking.

⭐ Quiz: Lessons 1–3, 5–8

✎Worksheet 933 marks
10 Above level Stars, Galaxies & Starlight Turn to page 42

Two parts — light-years, star types, stellar life cycles and galaxies; then the EM spectrum, telescopes and spectroscopy.

⭐ Quiz: Lessons 1–3, 5–9
