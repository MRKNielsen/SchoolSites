{\LARGE\bfseries\color{Ink} Desk trebuchet --- clip-together kit}\\[2pt]
{\large One sheet of 6\,mm plywood \textbullet{} no glue \textbullet{} slotted masses in a weight basket}\\[2pt]
{\color{RuleGrey}\rule{\linewidth}{1.2pt}}

A desk-sized trebuchet (400\,mm throwing arm) cut from \textbf{one 900 $\times$ 600 sheet of 6\,mm plywood} (every part sits inside the middle 850 $\times$ 550, leaving 25\,mm clear at each edge), that students clip together in about \textbf{10 minutes} and take apart flat again. There is \textbf{no glue}: the parts lock with hooked tabs, lock plugs, tapered keys and dovetails, and two bolts with wing nuts. The counterweight is a \textbf{plywood basket} that hangs from the arm and holds the school's slotted masses, so changing the counterweight takes seconds --- the variable changed in the investigation.

\parthead{Assembly}{two students \textbullet{} about 10 minutes \textbullet{} no glue, no tools}

\noindent\fcolorbox{LineGrey}{WarnBg}{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule}{\footnotesize
\textbf{Reading the drawings.}\quad \textcolor[HTML]{C96A00}{\textbf{Orange}} parts are the ones you add or move in that step; pale parts are already built.\quad
\rednum{Red numbers} match the numbered instructions.\quad A lettered circle is a close-up of the dashed circle with the same letter.}}

\step{1}{Check the parts}{steps/d01_inventory.pdf}{%
Count the parts against the drawing. The two sides are mirror images: \textbf{SIDE L} and \textbf{SIDE R} are engraved on them. The sheet also carries spares: 1 lock plug, 1 trigger plug, 4 tie keys, 2 large and 2 small washers, 2 sling rings.
\need{everything on the sheet, plus the two bolts, wing nuts, steel washers and cord.}}

\step{2}{Trough guides into the base}{steps/d02_guides.pdf}{%
Lay the base engraved side up, with FRONT pointing the way you will throw.
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0pt, leftmargin=1.5em, topsep=2pt]
  \item Drop the three hooked tabs of each trough guide through the slots down the middle of the base (close-up \textbf{A}). They go in \textbf{10\,mm forward} of where they finish.
  \item Slide each guide \textbf{10\,mm back}. The hooks go under the base.
\end{enumerate}
The \emph{pin} hole near the back of each guide is for the metal-rod release pin (step 5) --- nothing to do with it yet.
\need{base, 2 trough guides.}}

\step{3}{Build the frame}{steps/d03_frame.pdf}{%
Build the frame on the desk first, \emph{before} it goes on the base. The ties cannot be fitted once the sides are locked down.
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0pt, leftmargin=1.5em, topsep=2pt]
  \item Stand the two sides up, engraving facing \textbf{out}. Hold a tie between them at the front, and the other at the back.
  \item Push the sides together, so the tabs on the ties go through the slots in the legs and the legs rest against the ties' shoulders.
  \item Push a \textbf{key} through the hole in each tie end (close-up \textbf{B}): lying \textbf{flat}, thin end first, with its \textbf{straight edge} (the one with the engraved line) against the side. Push until it is firm.
\end{enumerate}
\need{2 sides, 2 ties, 4 keys.}}

\step{4}{Clip the frame to the base}{steps/d04_clip.pdf}{%
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0pt, leftmargin=1.5em, topsep=2pt]
  \item Lower the frame so all four foot hooks drop through the slots near the edges of the base. The feet go in \textbf{10\,mm forward} of where they finish.
  \item Slide the whole frame \textbf{10\,mm back}. Each hook goes under the base.
  \item Push a \textbf{lock plug} into the gap left in front of each foot. The frame can't slide or lift out now.
\end{enumerate}
\need{4 lock plugs.}}

\step{5}{Trigger}{steps/d05_trigger.pdf}{%
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0pt, leftmargin=1.5em, topsep=2pt]
  \item Drop the trigger post's hook through the small slot beside the trough, at the back of the base.
  \item Slide the post \textbf{10\,mm forward}. Its hook goes under the base.
  \item Push a \textbf{trigger plug} (shorter than the lock plugs, so it fits the narrower post) into the gap behind the post.
  \item Slide the trigger key through the slot in the post from the outside, so its handle stays outside. Tie a 1\,m pull cord to the handle.
\end{enumerate}
\need{trigger post, trigger key, 1 trigger plug, pull cord.}

\noindent\emph{Metal-rod alternative:} skip the post, key and plug, and instead push the steel rod through the \emph{pin} hole in both trough guides once the arm is cocked (step 9) --- it passes straight through the arm's \emph{trigger} hole between them. Pull the rod sideways to fire.}

\step{6}{Get the arm ready}{steps/d06_arm.pdf}{%
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0pt, leftmargin=1.5em, topsep=2pt]
  \item Slide the \textbf{15\textdegree{} release tip} into the dovetail at the end of the arm, with its prong pointing towards the sling side (close-up \textbf{C}).
  \item \textbf{Sling:} tie a 400\,mm cord through the \emph{sling} hole and to one end of a pouch. Tie a sling ring to the other end of the pouch with a short cord. The middle of the pouch should be 360\,mm from the tip.
  \item Tie a short loop of cord (about 120\,mm round) through the \emph{trigger} hole.
  \item Strap the sensor plate where the arm says \emph{sensor here}, and the sensor to it.
\end{enumerate}
\need{arm, 15\textdegree{} release tip, sling ring, pouch, cord, sensor plate.}}

\step{7}{Hang the basket on the arm}{steps/d07_basket.pdf}{%
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0pt, leftmargin=1.5em, topsep=2pt]
  \item Push the M6 bolt through the two \textbf{bails} and the hang hole at the short end of the arm, in the order shown: 5 small wooden washers each side of the arm.
  \item Push the floor's tabs into the bottom slots of both bails.
  \item Push each wall's tabs into the side slots of both bails. Then tighten the wing nut.
\end{enumerate}
\need{2 bails, floor, 2 walls, M6 bolt, wing nut, 2 steel washers, 10 small wooden washers.}}

\step{8}{Fit the arm}{steps/d08_axle.pdf}{%
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0pt, leftmargin=1.5em, topsep=2pt]
  \item Hold the arm upright between the sides, long end up, with the basket hanging below.
  \item Push the M8 bolt through the sides and the arm in the order shown (\textbf{D}): 9 large wooden washers each side of the arm. Tighten the wing nut.
\end{enumerate}
\need{M8 bolt, wing nut, 2 steel washers, 18 large wooden washers.}}

\parthead{Launching}{teacher supervises \textbullet{} safety glasses on}

{\centering\includegraphics[width=0.92\linewidth]{steps/d09_cocked.pdf}\par}\vspace{2pt}
{\small
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1pt, leftmargin=1.5em, topsep=2pt]
  \item Put the slotted masses in the basket, with the arm hanging at rest.
  \item \textbf{Cock it:} pull the tip of the arm down to the back. Hook the trigger loop over the trigger key, between the post and the trough (close-up \textbf{E}).
  \item \textbf{Load it:} lay the sling in the trough with a beanbag in the pouch, and drop the ring over the release tip.
  \item Everyone behind the trebuchet. The operator holds the pull cord from the side and calls \textbf{``Firing''}.
  \item Pull the cord: the key slides out and the loop lets go.
  \item Wait until the arm stops before anyone touches it.
\end{enumerate}}

\sechead{Tuning}
{\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{5.8cm} Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{What you see} & \textbf{What to change} \\
\midrule
Beanbag goes straight up, or backwards & Releases too early: swap to the \textbf{0\textdegree{}} release tip. \\
Beanbag hits the ground just in front & Releases too late: swap to a higher tip (30, 45, then 60\textdegree{}). \\
Beanbag flies off to one side & Sling cords uneven, or the beanbag isn't in the middle of the trough. \\
Arm stiff or slows quickly & Back the axle wing nut off a little; check the washers aren't pinched. \\
Frame rocks & Check the four keys and the four lock plugs are pushed fully home. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}}

\warn{\textbf{Safety.} Safety glasses for everyone near it. Launch along an empty corridor or hall, away from windows, with nobody in front of the trebuchet or within 1\,m of its sides. Beanbags only. Change the masses only with the arm at rest. Keep fingers out of the frame while it is cocked: the basket swings down hard.}

\parthead{Pack down}{about 5 minutes}
Masses out first. Then reverse the assembly: arm off the axle bolt; basket off the arm (keep the washers on the bolts so they don't get lost); lock plugs out, slide the frame forward and lift it off the base; keys out, ties out; trigger plug out, slide the trigger post forward and lift it out; slide each trough guide forward and lift it out. Everything packs flat --- store each kit in its own A3 envelope or tray with its bolts, cord, washers, tent pegs and spares.
