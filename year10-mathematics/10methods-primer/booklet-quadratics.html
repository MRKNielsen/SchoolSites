Why the two chapters share a booklet
Chapter 7 is Chapter 5 drawn on axes. Every sketching method in 7C, 7D and 7E is a factorising or solving method you already met in 5D–5J — if a Chapter 7 hurdle question stalls, the fix is usually back in Chapter 5, and having both in one file makes that a page turn rather than a hunt.
